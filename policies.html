Terms & Conditions

By booking a stay at Bomel Mountain Resort, you agree to the terms outlined on this page. These terms apply to every reservation made directly with us, whether by phone, email, or through this website.

Rates listed on this site are per night, in Philippine pesos, and are subject to change without prior notice for reservations not yet confirmed with a deposit. Once a deposit has been paid, your rate is locked in for that stay.

A confirmed booking is considered a contract between the guest and Bomel Mountain Resort. Misuse of the property, its facilities, or its staff may result in the reservation being cancelled without refund, at management's discretion.

We reserve the right to refuse service to anyone who has previously violated these terms or our house rules during a past stay.

House Rules & Regulations

A short list of what keeps the grounds enjoyable and safe for every guest staying with us.

  • Quiet hours: 9:00 PM – 7:00 AM

    Kept mainly around the cabins and lodge rooms, out of respect for guests turning in early for the trail.

  • Fires stay in the designated firepit

    No open flames elsewhere on the property, including cabin porches, due to fire risk on dry months.

  • Dogs on-leash, and only in permitted areas

    Welcome in the Pine House and on the public trails; not permitted in the Lodge or dining hall.

  • No smoking indoors

    Designated outdoor smoking areas are marked near the parking lot and firepit lounge.

  • Trail guests sign in at the front desk

    Required before any hike beyond the marked ridge loop, for basic safety tracking.

  • Damage is billed after checkout

    Any damage to rooms or shared facilities beyond normal wear is charged to the card on file.

Privacy Policy

We collect only what's needed to manage your reservation: your name, contact details, and stay dates. This information is used to confirm bookings, send stay-related updates, and respond to inquiries.

We do not sell or rent guest information to third parties. Information submitted through the newsletter signup on this site is used only to send occasional updates about trail conditions, seasonal rates, and resort news, and you can unsubscribe at any time.

Records of past stays are kept for accounting purposes and are not shared outside of Bomel Mountain Resort's own booking and front-desk systems.

Cancellation & Refund Policy

We know plans change, especially with mountain weather involved — here's exactly where you stand at each stage of a booking.

  • 5+ days before check-in: full refund of any deposit paid, no questions asked.
  • Inside 5 days of check-in: the deposit becomes non-refundable, but the date can be moved once, subject to availability.
  • No-shows: charged in full for the first night booked, with the rest of the reservation released.
  • Resort-initiated cancellations (severe weather, road closure) are refunded in full, no exceptions.
