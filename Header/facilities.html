Facilities

Everything you can use during your stay

Cottages and rooms to sleep in, a spring-fed pool to cool off in, and a handful of shared spaces spread across the grounds — all included, none of it behind an extra fee.

Places to stay

Cottages, cabins, and a couple of looser options

Ridge Cabins

Single-room timber cabins facing the ridgeline, each with a wood stove and a private porch.

Creekside Lodge Rooms

Ground-floor rooms a few steps from the water, sharing a porch that runs the length of the building.

Pine House Cottages

Standalone two-bedroom cottages with a kitchenette, built for families staying the full week.

Meadow Glamping Tents

Canvas tents on raised platforms in the lower meadow, each with a real bed and a private firepit.

Farm-to-Table Dining Hall

Wellness Lodge

Communal Firepit

The unofficial center of the resort after dinner.

Trail Map & Gear Room

Guided Morning Walks

Free ridge walks each morning, led by staff who grew up here.

Guest Laundry

Behind the lodge, useful for longer stays or muddy trail days.

Reading Nook & Library

Secondhand books and a wall of windows facing the valley.

Onsite Parking

Stargazing Deck

No light pollution up here — bring a blanket.
