Location

Find your way onto the mountain

Bomel Resort sits at the end of the public mountain road — no private toll gate, no members-only turnoff. Here's exactly how to get here, however you're arriving.

On the map

Brgy. San Vicente Tagkawayan Quezon, Mountain Public Road

Any vehicle can reach the front gate on the public road; a 4x4 is only recommended after heavy rain, when the last stretch can get soft.

Getting here

Three ways to arrive

By car

The most direct route. Paved road the whole way except the final 2km of gravel leading to the gate. Free onsite parking once you arrive.

By public transit

Provincial buses run daily to the Highland District terminal. From there, a short tricycle ride covers the remaining distance to the gate.

By motorcycle

The nearest airport is roughly 90 minutes from the base of the mountain road. Airport pickup can be arranged with the front desk in advance.
