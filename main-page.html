Why Bomel Resort?

Built into public land, not fenced off from it

Most of the mountain around us belongs to everyone — the trailheads, the ridge lookouts, the creek that runs past the lodge. Bomel Resort was built to sit inside that, not wall it off. Guests can walk straight from their cabin door onto the public trail network, no shuttle required.

Pools and rooms are simple and warm. Meals lean on what's grown nearby. And the loudest thing after 9pm is usually the creek.

On the grounds

Everything stays low and quiet

Nothing here is built taller than the treeline. The facilities are spread across the grounds so no single building dominates the view.

  • Trailhead access

    Direct gate onto 40km of public mountain trail, no vehicle needed.

  • Spring-fed pool

    Unheated, cold year-round, filled by the same creek that runs past the lodge.

  • Farm-to-table dining

    One shared menu each evening, built around what came in from nearby farms that day.

  • Guided walks

    Free ridge walks each morning, led by resort staff who grew up on this mountain.
